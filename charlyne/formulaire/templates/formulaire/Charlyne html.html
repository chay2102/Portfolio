About Me

I am Charlyne Yowa Tshikoza, a software engineer by training and a graduate researcher at Yokohama National University in Japan. Originally from Goma, Democratic Republic of the Congo, I am interested in the ways technology and engineering can respond to real human needs.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Université Lumière de Bujumbura. My background combines software development/engineering, networking, IT support, administration, human resources, and customer experience.

My current academic interests include motion control, haptics, and rehabilitation technology. I am especially interested in developing accessible and practical technologies that can support people and healthcare services, including in resource-constrained environments.

Beyond technology and research, I enjoy writing, discovering new places, and sharing experiences through digital content. I also have a book in progress, La plus belle course de ma vie.

You can find my videos on YouTube and my other platforms through Linktree.
